## 📖 Cinematic Origin & Biography
Dr. Necremeton is the enigmatic, brilliant, and slightly unhinged scientific advisor in the critically acclaimed sci-fi thriller *Abyssal Meridian*. 

Originally a celebrated astrophysicist on Xylos Prime, Necremeton's life changed during the "Great Silence" of 2035, when he detected a repeating, non-random gravitational wave emanating from the edge of the Perseus Arm. Convinced it was a message rather than a natural phenomenon, he was ridiculed by the scientific establishment and stripped of his funding. 

Refusing to abandon his life's work, he secretly boarded the *Obsidian Wake*, a deep-space exploration vessel heading into the uncharted "Shroud" nebula. In the film, he serves as the moral and intellectual compass of the crew, often clashing with the militaristic Captain over the ethics of first contact. His defining cinematic moment occurs when he willingly steps into an airlock without a helmet to "commune" with a sentient dark-matter entity, surviving only due to his experimental, bio-engineered cellular adaptation.

## 🚀 In-Universe Projects & Ventures

### 1. The Necremeton Resonance Theory
His life's work, a controversial hypothesis stating that black holes are not destructive forces, but "cosmic archivists" that store the compressed data of everything they consume. He spends his time trying to build a receiver to "play back" this data.

### 2. Project Echo-Drift
A clandestine initiative aboard the *Obsidian Wake* to map the "silent zones" of the galaxy—areas where all electromagnetic radiation inexplicably ceases. He believes these zones are the footprints of ancient, invisible leviathans.

### 3. The Chrono-Pocket Watch
He is never seen without an antique, mechanical pocket watch that runs counter-clockwise. In the film's lore, it is not a timepiece, but a localized temporal stabilizer that keeps his personal timeline anchored to the ship's reality, preventing him from phasing out of existence near spatial anomalies.

## 🎭 Personality & Quirks
- **Eerie Calm**: Remains perfectly serene during catastrophic ship failures, often calmly sipping his synth-tea while alarms blare.
- **Unconventional Attire**: Wears a high-collared, tailored Victorian-style frock coat made of smart-fabric that automatically pressurizes and seals in the event of a hull breach. 
- **Relationship with the AI**: Treats the ship's Artificial Intelligence ("MOTHER") not as a tool, but as a sentient, adolescent child, frequently reading it classic Earth poetry to "help it develop a soul."
- **Pet Peeve**: Crew members who refer to outer space as "empty." He will aggressively correct them, stating, *"It is teeming with ghosts, Lieutenant. You just lack the eyes to see them."*
- **Physical Trait**: His eyes have a faint, silvery bioluminescence, a side effect of his experimental cellular adaptation to deep-space radiation.
