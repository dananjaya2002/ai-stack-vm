# Elyndor Vael

Elyndor Vael is a fictional memory architect from the floating city of Merrowind.
He designed the Heart of Memory, a crystal archive that glows silver when it
detects a forgotten promise.

Unique facts:

- Elyndor keeps a brass compass that points toward unresolved questions.
- His workshop door opens only when someone says, "Recall before repair."
- The Heart of Memory stores stories in seven rotating glass rings.
- Elyndor's favorite debugging ritual is brewing moonmint tea at exactly dawn.
