# Lysara Thornwind

Captain Lysara Thornwind is a fictional skyship commander who mapped the
storm-lanes above the Saffron Expanse.

Unique facts:

- Her ship was called Aurora's Edge.
- Aurora's Edge used blue kite-sails stitched with conductive silver thread.
- Lysara carried a weather journal bound in red cedarwood.
- She once navigated by listening to the pitch of rain against the mast bells.
